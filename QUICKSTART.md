# TCM Train the Trainer — Student Quick-Start

*One page. Read this aloud at the start of class (about 1 minute).*

---

Welcome. Your course books for today are on your laptop — no paper copies needed.

**Open the link** we gave you. You'll see a welcome screen. Press **Start**.

**Pick your book** from the library on the left. The first page you see is the
book's cover, by itself — just like a closed book on a desk. Use the
**arrow buttons**, the **arrow keys** on your keyboard, or **click the edge of
a page** to turn pages. Inside, pages come in pairs, like a real open book,
and the last page — the back cover — is on its own again.

If you get lost, type a page number in the **Go to page** box at the top.
The number under each page is the book's real page number, so when the
instructor says "turn to page 42," that's the page to go to.

**To take notes**, use the toolbar at the bottom of the screen:

- The **pencil** adds a sticky note — click it, then click anywhere on a page.
- **Select text** with your mouse, pick a colour, and press
  **Highlight selection**.
- The **T** adds a text box you can type in, right on the page — handy for
  filling in blanks in the margins.
- Made a mistake? The curved **undo arrow** takes it back.

**One important thing:** your notes are saved automatically, but *only in
this browser, on this computer*. If you close with a private window, clear
your browser, or switch laptops, your notes will be gone.

So **before you leave today**, press **Save annotated PDF** on the left.
That gives you a copy of the book with all of your notes inside it — yours to
keep. You can also press **Back up my notes** to save a small file you can
restore later with **Restore notes**.

That's it. Open your first book, and let's begin.
